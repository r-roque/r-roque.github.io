About Me

Designer who codes.
Researcher who cares about people.

I'm a first-generation student at UNC Chapel Hill pursuing a B.S. in Information Science & Data Science — driven by a belief that the best digital products live at the intersection of technical rigor and human-centered design.

My technical work spans deep learning computer vision pipelines for cancer imaging research, protein structure analysis for computational biology, and predictive modeling for clinical outcomes. My design work spans UX systems, brand identity, and full-stack web platforms.

I'm drawn to roles where the data problem and the design problem are the same problem — and where the solution has to work for a real person, not just a benchmark.
